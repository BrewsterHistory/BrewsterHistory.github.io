This is a library of historic maps of Brewster, the Town of Southeast, and Putnam County, gathered from the Library of Congress and other public collections. The maps are reproduced here for research and local-history use; rights and credit information appears on each collection’s page.

Like the markers catalog, this is a reference section rather than a primary-source narrative. Where a map underlies deeper research published on this site, links to the relevant narrative are included alongside it.
